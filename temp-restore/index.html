Frequently asked questions

Parul University offers a range of hostel options, including single, double, and triple occupancy rooms, designed to suit different preferences and budgets. Both AC and non-AC accommodations are available for male and female students.

All hostels are located within the university campus, ensuring students stay connected to their classes, facilities, and activities while enjoying a secure residential environment.

Hostel rooms come furnished with essential amenities like a bed, study table, chair, and wardrobe, along with access to shared washrooms and common recreational areas.

Yes, nutritious meals are provided at on-campus dining halls. Students can choose from multiple cuisines and meal options, with vegetarian and hygienic preparations being a standard.

All hostels are equipped with high-speed Wi-Fi connectivity, enabling students to continue their academic and research activities seamlessly.

Round-the-clock security personnel, CCTV surveillance, and a digital gate pass system ensure complete safety and controlled access for all residents.

Each hostel features indoor games, TV lounges, and common rooms to promote relaxation, community engagement, and a healthy social life on campus.

Students may indicate roommate preferences during the application process, and the administration tries to accommodate them based on availability and compatibility.

Yes, all hostels offer convenient laundry services managed by trained staff, ensuring cleanliness and ease for students with busy schedules.

Students can apply for accommodation through the university’s official portal during admission. The hostel fee structure varies based on room type and amenities selected.
